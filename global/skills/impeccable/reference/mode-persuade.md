# Persuade and Experience

Rules for the directions in [new-work-world.md](new-work-world.md) and for the build. The design is what the visitor judges, so the world may own and structure the page.

## Directions
- **Persuade:** the opening makes the offer clear and desirable, shows something only this product can prove, and holds the category's real action in working form (an airline's route-and-dates search, a clinic's open slots, a store's add to cart), not a link to it. Conversion lives in the form's vocabulary: a one-line hook, a visible primary action, a legible reading order.
- **Experience:** the work itself leads from the first viewport.
- A direction whose source is an interface language may carry its grammar across navigation, content, controls and states, within platform conventions.

## Build
- The world carries the page, never a picture of it: a viewport painted whole as a facade or cover with no working parts fails, and so does the generic product page (headline, subcopy, button, a row of equal cards, or a split hero with a badge row) in the world's colours.
- Coverage: the world's material is the ground and major surfaces and its type sets the headlines; a world reduced to an accent is the default with a sticker. Depth: one dominant move plus supporting material, type and spacing; other regions stay in the material without performing it.
- The parts still work: navigation reads as links, copy as set text, the action is plainly clickable, text reads against its background, sections keep a steady rhythm. Fix a poster by making its parts work where the world put them, never by moving them into a standard layout.
- An object world is one object seen once, at one scale, in its real form (a book opens to two pages on one gutter); more content goes on its own surfaces or the next spread.
