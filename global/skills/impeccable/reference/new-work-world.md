# Create or replace the visual world

Step 3 of [new-work.md](new-work.md); step 4 follows. First read the mode's rules, which win over shared guidance, and reread them before judging the build: [mode-persuade.md](mode-persuade.md) (Persuade, Experience), [mode-operate.md](mode-operate.md) or [mode-read.md](mode-read.md).

When the user or brief names both the world and the first-surface experience in quotable words, skip to step 4 with FORM `pinned`. A product cited as a reference sets the craft bar, not the world.

1. **Ground it:** one sentence each on the product's unique mechanism, the audience's real scene, its cultural home, and what this surface must prove. Name the rut: the page this category always ships and its predictable opposite. A brief's own picture (product name, metaphor) joins the rut; at most one candidate on its literal reading.
2. **Seven candidates** from the audience's cultural world: artifacts, places, rituals, graphic and screen traditions (notation, publications, identity programmes, data graphics), nameable abstract systems. Each: one line on why it resonates and carries the mechanism, one on how it looks (ground and main colours, type voices, one signature). Rank by resonance. Ask what it would be as a physical object and how its world looked before the web. Near-duplicates count once; span at least three material families. Operate and Read: never the tools the audience operates (terminal, scope, control panel); Persuade and Experience: at most one.
3. **Directions:** each candidate becomes a visual world joined to a concrete first-surface experience, viable: every claim true, a real palette and component family, buildable within assets and performance budget. Replace one that fails on truth; missing demo data is authored and labelled synthetic, never a reason to drop a bold direction.
4. **Weigh:** the top-ranked is the lead; test each other on audience identification and product clarity. Beats it on both: new lead. Holds one: alternate. Loses both: declined, but name the one discipline it has that the lead lacks (palette commitment, density, structural honesty), raise the lead to match, and write each raise as a line naming its donor. Donate discipline, never clothes (a button style, a display face).
5. **Offer** with AskUserQuestion: the raised lead, at most one alternate, then the category standard played straight, last. Each: thesis, palette (ground, main colours, their jobs), type voices, first viewport, signature interaction, an honest risk. The owner picks or asks for a fresh set (drop all shown; after two, ask what is missing). Change the lead yourself only on factual grounds, never taste. Owner away: build the lead, record it with `wf assume`.

Never recommend the category standard or soften toward it. If the user takes it, ask for two or three products it should sit beside, match their craft at full fidelity without irony, and record a standing preference as a brand commitment in PRODUCT.md.

Pinned constraints and PRODUCT.md brand commitments beat your ranking; when a direction's materials clash with them, translate the materials and say so.
