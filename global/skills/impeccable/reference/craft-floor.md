# Craft floor

Build to this without announcing it. A pinned brief or the committed world overrides any line except a ban; your habit does not. The floor never picks the direction: with every check green, spend the page on the world, and when torn between refined and committed, commit.

## Verify, in each batched look at the render
- **Contrast:** body and placeholder text ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1. Secondary text on coloured surfaces is tinted from the hue or the foreground, never grey.
- **Depth:** shadows have an offset and a soft blur; a zero-offset coloured halo is decoration.
- **Spacing:** tight groups, generous separation, more space above a heading than below. Read computed values.
- **Type:** body 65–75ch, display ≤6rem, tracking ≥-0.04em, balanced headings, clear scale and weight steps. Run the real copy at every breakpoint and fix overflow.
- **Motion:** one authored moment, not scattered effects or one entrance on every section. Exponential ease-out from an already-visible default. Blur, backdrop-filter, clip-path, mask and shadow are fair game when smooth.
- **States:** hover, disabled, loading, error, empty; real content, working controls, responsive composition, keyboard focus.
- **Browser surfaces:** theme text selection, caret, scrollbars, focus rings, underline offset and tabular numerals from the palette; models skip these most.
- **Copy:** the product's own language; controls name their action; errors name the problem and the recovery.
- **Coverage:** every brief requirement present and findable within seconds.

## Refuse
Category defaults the brief's own words can earn back; a **ban** never comes back. Reaching for one on a free axis means rewrite the element, not soften it.
- Same-size icon, heading and text cards as the page structure; nested cards, ever.
- The hero-metric template: big number, small label, supporting stats, accent.
- **Ban:** a kicker or eyebrow above a heading.
- Section numbers (01 / 02 / 03) unless the sequence informs the reader.
- A modal where no interruption or protected focus is needed.
- Gradient text; emphasis comes from weight or size.
- Glass and blur as decoration.
- A coloured `border-left` or `border-right` over 1px on cards, list items, callouts or alerts.
- Hard offset shadows (`box-shadow: 4px 4px 0`) outside a truly neobrutalist world.
- Sparklines, progress rings or soft rounded rectangles standing in for content.
- Monospace as a "technical" costume rather than for code, data or measurement.
- A system display face (Impact, Arial Black, the platform sans) as an own-world page's display voice: source and self-host a face of the right character.
- Unicode glyphs or emoji as icons: a real library or authored SVG, one stroke and weight.
- Geometric masks (circle, polygon, radial cutout) faking a photo subject's edge: derive an alpha matte or a cut-out asset.
- Light or dark by category instead of the use scene.
